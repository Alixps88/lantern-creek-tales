Lantern Creek Tales

Complete frontier love stories · a real ending · a little true history

Lantern Creek Tales is a YouTube channel of clean, full-length western romance stories set in the fictional town of Lantern Creek, Wyoming Territory, 1869–1889. Each story runs 40–50 minutes, is told by a calm narrator over public-domain American paintings and photographs, and always ends happily.

Watch on YouTube

Narration is AI-generated, and each video is labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube. Every story is original. Artwork comes from public-domain museum collections and is credited in each video description.
